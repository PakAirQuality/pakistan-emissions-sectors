# rc67 baseline for the integrated inventory campaign

This immutable snapshot preserves the inspected release at commit
`3db0d658294dae1e6c760844df167daf7938fd65` before the rc68 scientific rebase.
`baseline.json` and `SHA256SUMS` identify every preserved input and product.
Optional products absent in the inspected release are declared rather than fabricated.
The baseline is historical evidence, not the revised inventory. Its spatial databases
retain the licences of their upstream inputs, including ODbL where applicable, and
are excluded from the compatible primary emissions deposit.
