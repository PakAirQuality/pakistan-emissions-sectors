# Committed proxy rasters — provenance and rebuild

Six 0.05° (267×340) float32 rasters (five allocation proxies and, since rc41, the
GCD-Rice crosscheck instrument), cached from their primary sources by
`src/pkinv/core/gis/proxies.py` and committed so the gridded cube
(`pkinv.report.grid_cube`) is offline-deterministic and CI-runnable. Each `.npy` carries a
`.npy.txt` description written at build time; `proxies.get_proxy()` refuses a cache whose
shape disagrees with the grid.

| cache | source | vintage | licence | rebuild |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `proxy_population_267x340.npy` | WorldPop constrained population, 1 km, aggregated | 2020 | CC-BY 4.0 | `proxies.get_proxy("population", grid, use_cache=False)` (reads the local `worldpop_pak_2020_1km.tif`, tracked since rc10, sha256 `d24169128fef55cbe288e73887921d11d1384a53e873d234a68e3f8898f7cdbb` — the one proxy input a re-derivation cannot do without) |
| `proxy_builtup_267x340.npy` | ESA WorldCover built-up class, via /vsicurl | 2021 | CC-BY 4.0 | same, name `builtup` (network) |
| `proxy_cropland_267x340.npy` | ESA WorldCover cropland class | 2021 | CC-BY 4.0 | same, name `cropland` (network) |
| `proxy_glw_livestock_267x340.npy` | FAO Gridded Livestock of the World 4, EF-weighted CTL/BFL/SHP/GTS | 2020 | CC-BY 4.0 | same, name `glw_livestock` (network) |
| `proxy_manure_applied_267x340.npy` | derived: per-cell cropland × livestock | derived | derived from the two above | same, name `manure_applied` |

Two further proxies are MANUAL ACQUISITIONS, both now HELD (their former absence
classes are deleted from the allow-list, so a checkout without either refuses the
fixpoint - the reverse of a silent substitution):

- `spam_rice` — MapSPAM 2020 rice physical area, HELD since Wave 5 (rc26):
  `data/gis/manual/spam_rice.tif`, the V2r2 release of the product
  `docs/DATA_ACQUISITION.md` §2 describes (the doc named v1r0; same product line,
  Harvard Dataverse DOI, hashes in `data/gis/manual/RICE_SOURCES.json`). The
  `raster/spam_rice/cropland` fallback class was DELETED from the allow-list when
  the file landed — the firms precedent — so a checkout without it refuses the
  fixpoint. The own-year GCD-Rice 2023 raster beside it
  (`gcd_rice_2023.tif`) is the crosscheck instrument, never the surface.
- `firms_fire` — VIIRS fire detections for residue-burning timing/placement, HELD
  since rc13 (period-matched) and extended to the nine-year record at rc24
  (`data/gis/manual/firms_fire.csv`, sha256 in `FIRMS_SOURCES.json`); its
  cropland-fallback class was deleted when the extraction landed.

The caches are inputs like any other data file: regenerating one changes a committed file
and shows in a diff. They are NOT regenerated by CI or by `make cube`.

## Digests and vintage offsets (rc7 provenance closure)

Every cache is hashed into the release manifest's `sources[]` list; the
digests below let a re-derivation prove identity without the manifest.
All rasters predate the FY2023-24 reporting period: **the built-up and
cropland surfaces observe 2021 and population 2020, a declared vintage
offset of two to three years against the activity they allocate** - a
versioned spatial proxy, never a live measurement of the reporting period.
The built-up cache is ESA WorldCover v200 2021, class 50 (built-up), 34
tiles aggregated from 10 m to 0.05 degrees.

raster computed from OpenStreetMap geometry is a **derivative database** under ODbL 1.0
(share-alike; © OpenStreetMap contributors), not a produced work, and CC-BY cannot be
granted over it. See `LICENSE-data` for the repository-wide list.
