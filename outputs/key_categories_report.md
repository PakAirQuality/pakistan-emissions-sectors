# Key Category Analysis (IPCC 2006 Vol 1 Ch 4, Approach 1)

A **key category** has a significant influence on the national inventory's total **level**, **trend**, or both. Approach 1 flags categories cumulatively to the **95%** threshold on absolute (unsigned) CO2e (AR5 GWP-100).

- **Level assessment:** reference year 2024, full AFOLU set (6 agriculture categories + LULUCF forest & mangrove).
- **Trend assessment:** 1990 -> 2024, agriculture categories (the consistent time series). LULUCF is level-only here (no historical series - a documented limitation).

| Category | Level % | Cumulative % | Key (level) |
|---|---:|---:|:--:|
| Enteric fermentation | 67.9 | 67.9 | **KEY** |
| Managed soils | 12.8 | 80.6 | **KEY** |
| Manure management | 9.5 | 90.1 | **KEY** |
| Rice cultivation | 7.4 | 97.5 | **KEY** |

## Key categories (level OR trend)

- **Enteric fermentation** (level, trend)
- **Managed soils** (level, trend)
- **Manure management** (level, trend)
- **Rice cultivation** (level, trend)

_Interpretation: key categories are where methodological rigour, better activity data, and uncertainty reduction pay off most - consistent with the Monte-Carlo uncertainty ranking (`uncertainty_montecarlo.csv`)._
