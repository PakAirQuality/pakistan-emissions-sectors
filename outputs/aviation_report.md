## Memo items, excluded from the national total

- **International aviation LTO emissions at Pakistani airports** — 210.4 Gg CO2. IPCC 2006 GL Vol 2 Ch 3 s3.6.1: emissions from international aviation are excluded from national totals and reported separately as memo items. Covers only landing and take-off at Pakistani airports. It is distinct from the full-flight bunker memo estimated by the fuel bridge.
- **Non-commercial movements, LTO portion** — 7.8 Gg CO2. Reported separately rather than inside 1A3a: the CAA's non-commercial column mixes general aviation, training circuits and military movements at joint-use airfields, and IPCC reports military aviation under 1A5b.

## Fuel-control comparison

The OCAC customer labels are not treated as IPCC boundaries. Under the flight-fuel-weighted bridge, domestic aviation is 292.0 Gg CO2 versus 298.9 Gg in the unconstrained movement model (factor 0.977).

Commercial JP-1 implies 2032.1 Gg CO2 versus OECD's matched domestic plus international territory total of 2047.8 Gg (ratio 0.992). OECD supplies the central split and is therefore a calibration input, not independent validation.
