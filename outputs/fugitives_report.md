# Pakistan oil-and-gas fugitives, FY2023-24

Reference-configuration IPCC-2019 estimate: **23.499 Mt CO2e (AR5)**.
Parametric 95% interval: **21.277-30.537 Mt** (bounded input ranges, structural scenarios separate).
Legacy 2006 production-only benchmark: **3.805 Mt** for FY2019-20.
Pakistan's official 2022 CRT reports **4.433 Mt** for 1.B.2.

The difference is not closed by assumption. It is decomposed by segment, practice, activity basis, coverage and observations in the generated products. UFG is diagnostic only; VIIRS flare volume and UNEP MARS events are evaluation layers and are not double-counted into inclusive Tier-1 factors.
