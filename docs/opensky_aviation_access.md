# OpenSky historical-data access specification

This repository does not store OpenSky credentials or redistribute permissioned records.
PAQI should submit the following scope through OpenSky's institutional/non-profit access
route using an authorised organisational account.

## Research purpose

Construct a reproducible, fuel-constrained civil-aviation emissions inventory for Pakistan
for FY2023-24. The work evaluates ADS-B completeness against Pakistan Civil Aviation
Authority airport and airline control totals, models aircraft fuel with OpenAP, and reports
only aggregate or appropriately derived research products.

## Requested data

- `flights_data4`, 2023-07-01 through 2024-06-30, where estimated departure or arrival
  airport begins with `OP`;
- `state_vectors_data4` for the matched aircraft and flight intervals;
- aircraft ICAO24 metadata needed to assign registration and ICAO type designator;
- documentation of known database outages during the requested period.

The exact executable SQL and its checksum are generated by
`pkinv.sectors.aviation.opensky.query_manifest()`. Raw records will remain access-controlled
and will not be committed to the public repository. Published reproducibility materials will
contain queries, filters, manifests, coverage diagnostics, model code, and aggregate outputs.

## Contact details still required before submission

The authorised applicant must supply PAQI's legal name, institutional email, responsible
researcher, affiliation, intended publication venue, project duration, and agreement to the
current OpenSky terms. These identity and contractual fields are intentionally not guessed by
the codebase.
