# Oil Companies Advisory Council (OCAC)

**Subject:** Monthly petroleum sales by sector, product and province, July 2015 - June 2021

We already hold and use the OCAC sector/product/province monthly tables from
July 2021 onward, and they are the single most load-bearing monthly series in
this inventory. The six fiscal years before that are the largest remaining gap.

Requested: the final monthly sector x product x province petroleum sales tables
for July 2015 through June 2021, covering at minimum the consuming sectors Road,
Railways, Domestic Aviation (PIA and Others), Industry, Power and Agriculture,
and the products MS, HSD, JP-1, kerosene, LDO and furnace oil.

Also requested: stock adjustments, the treatment of refinery and marketing own
use, and any change in customer-class definitions during the period.

Why it matters: without it, six of nine years carry road, rail, domestic
aviation, industrial liquid fuels and liquid-fuel power on a flat profile, which
this inventory declares as a gap rather than concealing.

This request is made for a non-commercial national greenhouse-gas inventory
(PakEmis-GHG), prepared for submission to a peer-reviewed data journal. The
inventory is reproducible and every input is published with its provenance.

What we ask for in addition to the data itself, because a series without them
cannot be used honestly:

  * the unit and the reference conditions for every quantity;
  * whether each figure is provisional, revised or final, and the revision date;
  * the definition of every sector or customer class used;
  * any break in the series definition within the period;
  * permission to publish NORMALISED MONTHLY WEIGHTS (each year summing to one)
    and aggregated derived tables. We do not need permission to republish your
    tables themselves, and we will not do so; a monthly *shape* is sufficient for
    the inventory and is what we would cite you for.

If a subset is easier to release than the whole, please send the subset — a
partial series with its limits stated is more useful to us than none.
