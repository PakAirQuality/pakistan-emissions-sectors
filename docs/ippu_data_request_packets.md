## IPPU-CEM-01 — national clinker material balance

Dear APCMA / producer data team,

We are compiling a reproducible greenhouse-gas inventory for industrial processes in Pakistan. For
FY2023–24, please provide plant- or company-level clinker and cement flows in physical tonnes:
opening clinker stock, clinker production, clinker purchases, clinker imports, clinker ground,
domestic clinker sales, clinker exports, closing clinker stock, and cement production. Please mark
intercompany transfers and state whether the reporting boundary is a plant or consolidated company.

The data will be used to reconcile clinker production, trade, stocks and grinding. Missing values
may be left blank and explained; they will not be inferred silently.

## IPPU-CRT-01 — official calendar-2022 cement method

Dear national inventory team,

Please provide the source and methodological derivation for the calendar-2022 cement row in
Pakistan’s Common Reporting Tables, including the definition of the reported 50.2455 Mt activity,
whether it represents cement or clinker, the clinker-to-cement ratio, imports and exports, stock
change, cement-kiln-dust correction, carbonate or clinker chemistry, emission-factor source, and the
worksheet formula yielding the reported implied factor of 0.55539 t CO2 per tonne of activity.

The purpose is to reproduce the institutional method on its submitted boundary, not to replace it
with the study’s FY2023–24 activity series.

## IPPU-CHEM-01 — ammonia and nitric-acid plants

Dear NFDC / fertilizer producer data team,

For each ammonia and nitric-acid installation, please provide FY2023–24 and calendar-2022 actual
production and process information: ammonia production, feedstock and feedstock consumption, gross
process CO2, CO2 recovered into urea, other recovered or sold CO2, permanent storage, nitric-acid
production on a 100%-acid basis, process technology, N2O abatement technology, annual abatement
utilisation, destruction efficiency, and measured N2O where available. Please state whether each
record is fiscal or calendar year and distinguish feedstock gas from combustion fuel.

Capacity alone will be retained as plant evidence but will not be converted to annual emissions.

## IPPU-HFC-01 — species/application chemical flows

Dear National Ozone Unit / Pakistan Customs data team,

Please provide annual calendar-year HFC records for 2015–2025 by chemical or resolved blend and by
application: imports, exports, domestic production, destruction, new-equipment charge, servicing
charge, recovered chemical and retired charge. Preferred applications are domestic, commercial,
industrial and transport refrigeration; stationary and mobile air conditioning; foams; aerosols;
fire protection; and solvents.

Please report tonnes of chemical, not tonnes CO2-equivalent. Supply, equipment charge and servicing
are separate concepts in the inventory and will not be summed without an application-specific bank
model.
