# Ready-to-send data request letters

Drafts for the request lanes in `data/monthly_data_requests.csv` (status
`drafted_not_sent`). Each letter is self-contained; attach nothing. The register
row carries the machine-readable scope; these are the human texts. Nothing here
has been sent — sending is a maintainer action.

The stakes quoted in `docs/monthly_data_requests.md` regenerate from the temporal
decomposition, so quote that file at send time rather than copying numbers here.

## PBS — the one missing minerals year (rides lane MON-PETDIV-01)

**To:** Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, via the online data-request portal

Subject: Request for the monthly mineral production tables, July 2015 – June 2016

We maintain a public, reproducible national greenhouse-gas inventory for Pakistan
(PakEmis). PBS's monthly mineral production tables (crude oil, natural gas, coal,
limestone, by province) are a core input: we have ingested the published tables
for FY2016-17 through FY2023-24 from the Energy & Mining portal, each parsed
against its own printed totals. The one year absent from the current archive is
FY2015-16. Could PBS supply the July 2015 – June 2016 monthly mineral production
tables (the same publication series), in CSV or as the publication PDF? This
single year completes a nine-year panel; we request nothing else.

## NHA — station-month traffic counts (lane MON-NHA-01)

**To:** National Highway Authority, Road Asset Management Directorate

Subject: Request for vehicle-class traffic counts by station and month

We maintain a public national emissions inventory whose road-transport emissions
are allocated within provinces on road-network weights derived from
OpenStreetMap. NHA's toll, weigh-station and traffic-count systems record the
observed quantity those weights approximate. Could NHA supply vehicle-class
counts by station and month — station identifier and location, month, vehicle
class, count, direction if recorded — for any covered part of July 2015 to June
2024? No revenue or personal data is requested. The data would validate or
replace modelled allocation weights in a published, freely licensed dataset,
with NHA credited as the source.

## National Ozone Unit / FBR — species-resolved HFC detail (lane REQ-NOU-01)

**To:** National Ozone Unit, Ministry of Climate Change; cc FBR (Customs)

Subject: Request for species-resolved HFC consumption detail underlying
Pakistan's Article 7 reporting

The UNEP Ozone Secretariat's country data publishes Pakistan's aggregate Annex-F
(HFC) consumption for 2019, 2020, 2024 and 2025. We maintain a public national
greenhouse-gas inventory that currently carries F-gases as not-estimated, with
the aggregate consumption used only as a screening bound, because an emissions
estimate under the IPCC Guidelines requires detail the public aggregate does not
expose. Could the NOU supply, for calendar years 2015–2026 as available: imports,
production, exports, destruction and feedstock use by individual HFC or blend
(with blend compositions); end-use or licensing classification; imports and
exports of pre-charged equipment; and any servicing, recovery or destruction
records? 2021–2023, unreported in the public table, are the most valuable years.
The detail would let the published inventory replace a screening bound with a
proper equipment-bank estimate, credited to the NOU.

## CPPA-G / NEPRA — the power settlement residual (lane MON-CPPAG-01)

**To:** CPPA-G (Market Operations); cc NEPRA (Registrar)

Subject: Request for plant-month energy purchase data — residual items

We reconstruct plant-month generation from the published record: PBS electricity
generation tables (FY2015-16 – FY2020-21) and NEPRA State of Industry tables and
FCA filings for later years. For K-Electric specifically: the KE monthly FCA determinations reference a "plant wise generation statistics sheet" submitted with each filing that the published decisions do not annex - we request exactly that sheet, per month from July 2015, giving KE own generation by plant and fuel separate from CPPA purchases. The residual we cannot resolve from publications:
(1) monthly FUEL QUANTITIES by plant and fuel where the published exhibits carry
only energy and cost; (2) net-versus-gross generation definitions per plant;
(3) fuel NCV and heat-rate assumptions used in settlement where absent from the
filing; (4) K-Electric's own-generation monthly detail outside the XWDISCO
boundary. Machine-readable extracts of the monthly Energy Purchase Data exhibits
for July 2015 – June 2024 would settle all four; we hold and hash the public
documents already and request only what they omit.

## Oil Companies Advisory Council — historical monthly editions (lane MON-OCAC-01)

**To:** The Secretary General, Oil Companies Advisory Council, Karachi

Subject: Monthly petroleum sales by customer class, July 2015 to June 2021

We maintain a public, reproducible greenhouse-gas inventory for Pakistan built from
official records (PakEmis). OCAC's monthly editions from July 2021 onward time four of
our sectors; the six earlier fiscal years are carried flat because the corresponding
monthly editions are not on ocac.org.pk. We request the monthly sales-by-customer-class
tables (the same table the current editions carry: product x customer class, with the
provincial area pages where produced) for July 2015 through June 2021, in any format
convenient - the printed editions are entirely sufficient. We already hold and cite the
annual Pakistan Oil Reports for these years; it is only the monthly grain we lack.

## OGRA, SNGPL, SSGC and PPIS/DGPC — the gas system's monthly physical ledger (lane MON-GAS-01)

**To:** Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority, Islamabad; Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Ltd;
Sui Southern Gas Company Ltd; Petroleum Division (Directorate General Petroleum
Concessions / PPIS)

Subject: Monthly physical gas volumes, July 2015 to June 2024

For a national methane and CO2 inventory we request the monthly PHYSICAL ledger of the
gas system - volumes, not prices: system inlet (indigenous by field where available, and
RLNG separately), transmission throughput and compressor own-use, distribution input and
customer-class deliveries, UFG as booked, linepack change, and flared or vented volumes
with the GCV basis stated. The inventory currently times gas distribution and post-meter
sources on a flat profile for want of exactly these series; each is a named row in our
public request register with the emissions mass it would move.

## Municipal waste authorities and WASAs — weighbridge and wastewater monthlies (lane MON-MUNI-01)

**To:** Lahore Waste Management Company; Sindh Solid Waste Management Board; the
provincial local government departments; the Water and Sanitation Agencies (Lahore,
Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Hyderabad, Karachi Water Board)

Subject: Monthly disposal tonnage and wastewater loading

For the waste sector's methane estimate we request, for any facilities and years held:
monthly weighbridge tonnage by disposal site; transfer-station throughput; recovery or
composting quantities; and for the WASAs, monthly influent flow, BOD or COD where
sampled, population served, and treated-versus-bypass split. A single city's record is
useful: we publish per-city evidence and never scale one city to the country.

## Hydrocarbon Development Institute of Pakistan — Energy Yearbook fuel balances (lane REQ-HDIP-01)

**To:** Director General, HDIP, Islamabad

Subject: Full fuel-balance detail behind the Pakistan Energy Yearbook

Our inventory's largest declared gap is industrial fuel combustion (about 29 Mt CO2
against an external comparator). Closing it requires the Yearbook's fuel balances at
their finest industrial disaggregation: production, imports, exports, stock changes and
sectoral disposition per fuel, with captive-generation and feedstock quantities
identifiable. We request these tables for FY2015-16 through FY2023-24, ideally as
spreadsheets; we already purchase and cite the printed Yearbook.

## Pakistan Bureau of Statistics — CMI industry-by-fuel tables (lane REQ-CMI-01)

**To:** Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Islamabad

Subject: Census of Manufacturing Industries fuel-consumption tables

We request the CMI 2015-16 (and any later round's) industry-by-fuel consumption tables -
quantity and value by PSIC division - or the corresponding microdata under the PBS
microdata access policy. The QIM, which we already parse and cite, indexes production
but cannot classify fuel energy by branch; the CMI is the record our industrial fuel
ledger's activation gate names.

## National Fertilizer Development Centre — plant-level production and gas separation (lane REQ-NFDC-02)

**To:** Director, NFDC, Islamabad

Subject: Plant-wise production and feed/fuel gas split

We already parse the Annual Fertilizer Review's Annex-28 (plant gas consumption) and the
monthly offtake annexes. For an ammonia-sector carbon ledger we further request, per
plant and year (FY2015-16 to FY2023-24): ammonia and urea production, gas consumed as
feedstock versus as fuel, and any CO2 recovered or sold. The feedstock split is the one
quantity the published annexes do not separate.

## Provincial EPAs and operators — IPPU facility operating data (lane REQ-IPPU-01)

**To:** The provincial Environmental Protection Agencies; operators named per category
in our public scope registry (nitric acid, lime, glass, primary steel)

Subject: Facility operating data for process-emissions estimation

For the industrial-process categories our inventory does not yet estimate, we request
per facility and year: output by product, operating days, abatement equipment and its
actual operational status (for nitric acid), carbonate and cullet inputs (lime, glass),
and production route (steel). Screening estimates remain outside our published totals
until this evidence supports them; each category's activation gate is public in the
inventory's scope registry.
