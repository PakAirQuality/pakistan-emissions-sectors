\section*{S3.1 Conditional parameter calculation}

The current annual conditional product reruns native agricultural quantities from
shared physical inputs. It does not sample unrelated category totals and then
infer a material balance from their sum. Each species' sampled herd enters
enteric methane, manure nitrogen and the linked ledger-L household dung quantity.
Each crop production draw enters its residue destinations; fertiliser nutrient
and product inputs retain their own source uncertainty and units. Synthetic
fertiliser NH$_3$ factors retain the adopted product mix and receive one common
table-wide factor multiplier in this conditional case; this is not a measurement
of cross-product factor covariance. Other dependence interpretations remain an
uncertainty limitation.

Nitrogen calculations retain the selected TAN or complete IPCC formulation.
The same surviving material state supplies management and post-application
NH$_3$, soil NO$_x$ and direct/indirect N$_2$O. Fractions drawn within this
conditional model retain their physical bounds. Management-system shares and
structural factor applicability are held within a case rather than independently
multiplied outside a partition. Excretion rates and unsupported management changes
remain stated assumptions. A wider interval does not repair a transcription
error or represent an unestimated source.

The reference assigns 0.95 of volatilized nitrogen to NH$_3$ under the adopted
chemical attribution. A separate deterministic case assigns all volatilized
nitrogen to NH$_3$ and reruns the linked NH$_3$, NO$_x$ and N$_2$O pathways from
the same native ledger. It tests attribution sensitivity without assigning a
probability or interpreting the endpoint as an uncertainty bound. The 0.80
irrigated/leaching applicability setting is likewise a conditional assumption;
approximate national area totals do not independently establish that fraction.

\section*{S3.2 Temporal dependence and fitness}

Historical changes use paired realizations. A persistent-parameter case retains
common uncertain factors across years, while a separate annual-activity-error
case tests a different activity dependence. Each change is calculated within a
realization before percentiles are taken. Subtracting unrelated annual percentiles
would give a different quantity and is not the reported trend interval.

The conditional intervals cover admitted sources under a named configuration.
Unestimated categories, alternative livestock or management reconstructions,
controls, destination choices and temporal/spatial allocation uncertainty are not
combined into a probability interval. The reference uncertainty year remains
FY2023--24 even though supported annual activity extends through FY2024--25.
The earlier component-lognormal sector diagnostic and Approach~1 calculations
retain their own settings when shown; they are not substituted for this native
annual product.
