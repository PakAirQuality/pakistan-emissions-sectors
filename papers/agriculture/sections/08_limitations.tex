\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
Livestock levels are reconstructed consistently around the adopted census, but annual fiscal head counts are not annual enumerations. Manure-system shares, nitrogen excretion and the destination of diverted dung remain structurally uncertain. Reference S receives the observed household fuel quantity; unresolved diverted material remains outside an assigned combustion fate. L assumes all diverted material is burned in households. A closed transfer does not establish the destination.

Tier-1/default factors and selected country-specific parameters do not constitute Pakistani field measurements. The direct non-rice soil factor's dry-climate transfer is consequential in irrigated cropland; rice and grazing require separate assessment. The aggregate soil-factor alternative is published without tuning to FAOSTAT. Continuous flooding is a rice-water-regime assumption rather than an annual management observation.

Fertiliser offtake is distinct from application. Product composition and nutrient controls are reconciled, but application dates, soil conditions and manure placement remain imperfectly observed. Regional urea factors and source-stage TAN factors may transfer differently under Pakistan's conditions. A common nitrogen ledger prevents incompatible pathways; it does not validate those factors.

Crop production constrains residue supply. Burned, returned, household-fuel and unresolved residue destinations are distinct. Burning shares, combustion completeness, nitrogen contents and emissions factors remain uncertain; fire detections do not directly measure mass burned. Agricultural machinery and tillage/harvest dust lack admitted air estimates.

Conditional uncertainty samples shared inputs and bounded fate parameters. Fixed management shares, structural factor choices, unestimated categories and geographic/timing uncertainty are excluded from those percentiles and shown separately. Reference-year spatial patterns and monthly proxies are labelled as allocations. National source magnitudes cannot be interpreted as ambient concentration or exposure rankings. The inventory remains non-LULUCF; exploratory land-sector material is outside the joint national totals.
