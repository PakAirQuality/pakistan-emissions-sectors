Housing, storage and yards contribute about 838 Gg \NHthree{}, applied manure 815 Gg, synthetic fertiliser 737 Gg and pasture deposition 218 Gg in the reference year. Crop burning adds about 9.3 Gg. These stages act on the same conserved pools as agricultural \NtwoO{}. The air \NOx{} total includes management and grazing losses as well as synthetic, organic and crop-residue soil sources and burning; excluding management/grazing \NOx{} while retaining their volatilised nitrogen in the \NtwoO{} calculation would describe different ledgers.

\subsection{Temporal and spatial support}\label{sec:ap-spatial}\label{sec:ap-timeseries}\label{sec:ap-crosswalk}
Livestock housing/storage follows livestock support; applied manure follows the livestock--cropland intersection; synthetic fertiliser follows nutrient/cropland support. Field burning and household residue combustion use separate locations and activities. The 0.05$^\circ$ cells are allocations on those supports, not observations at that resolution. Placed plus unallocated mass closes to each pollutant's compatible control.

Manure-stage, grazing and residue-decomposition emissions have no observed monthly release series and use declared days-weighted allocation. Fertiliser offtake supplies a timing proxy, not observed application or volatilisation. Crop-burning fire evidence is a seasonal/location proxy and does not measure the national burned fraction. Temporal and spatial evidence shares are pollutant-specific. Newly extended annual activity does not imply newly observed monthly geography.

\subsection{Conditional and structural uncertainty}\label{sec:ap-structural}\label{tab:apstruct}
The integrated conditional ensemble uses the same realised herd, crop production, nutrient offtake and applicable nitrogen-fate factors for \NHthree{}, \NOx{} and \NtwoO{}. Bounded fractions prevent negative remaining material or emission fractions above one. Trend intervals use paired years, with persistent and annually independent activity-error cases kept separate. The intervals do not include alternative manure-management shares, excretion rates, climate-factor choices, missing sources or allocation uncertainty.

The TAN reference and IPCC alternative each regenerate their nitrogen account. Regional urea-factor and manure-management sensitivities remain structural cases without assigned probabilities. Agreement with FAOSTAT or another inventory is not a selection rule for a soil factor. The dry-climate non-rice factor is a declared reference whose applicability to irrigated cropland is imperfectly established; rice and grazing retain their own formulations.

\subsection{Matched external evidence}\label{sec:ap-benchmark}
The original public HTAPv3.2 NH$_3$ country workbook provides annual and monthly source controls without a private account. Pakistan's agriculture combines livestock, crops and agricultural waste burning. Exact July--June sums support comparisons through FY2019--20; later fiscal years are blank because the released HTAP series ends in calendar 2020. For FY2019--20, S\_TAN agriculture is 2215 Gg against 2184 Gg in HTAP, a ratio of 1.014. This is a sector-boundary comparison, not independent verification of individual pathways.

The HTAP mosaic uses REAS for much of South Asia and EDGAR for gap filling, agricultural burning and post-2015 trends. Shared inputs and trend extensions limit independence. Differences are interpreted by activity, livestock fate, product-specific fertiliser factors and coverage. No parameter is adjusted solely to reproduce the external total. Spatial coincidence with a proxy used in both products is not independent validation, and satellite ammonia columns require transport and retrieval treatment before they can constrain emissions.
