\subsection{Spatial products}\label{sec:results-proxies}\label{sec:results-district}\label{sec:results-districtyear}
Livestock, cropland, nutrient, rice and fire supports allocate different processes. Administrative controls and placed/unallocated mass retain their own boundaries. A fixed source surface changes magnitude with activity but does not establish changing local activity. The grid's nominal 0.05$^\circ$ spacing describes the allocation product, not an observational resolution. Independent district crop records test selected crop patterns; livestock counts used to construct a proxy cannot validate that same proxy.

\subsection{Temporal change and uncertainty}
The common series covers ten fiscal years, July 2015--June 2025. Legacy 1990--2024 calendar diagnostics remain separately labelled and are not spliced onto the new census reference to make a common national series. Trends use paired realisations and shared source revisions. Conditional uncertainty reflects sampled inputs and fate factors; management shares, factor applicability, material destinations and geographic/timing uncertainty are structural or unestimated limitations.

\subsection{Nitrogen and residue accounting}\label{sec:results-nledger}
TAN-reference stage losses determine surviving manure pools, management and grazing NH$_3$/NO$_x$ and the corresponding indirect N$_2$O. The IPCC alternative regenerates the same products on its separate parameter formulation. The two configurations are not combined into one probabilistic interval. Residue supply is partitioned among returned, field-burned, household-fuel and unresolved destinations. Closing those accounts does not observe their split. Household dung reference S receives the survey-supported quantity; alternative L assigns all diverted material to household combustion.

The air results in Section~\ref{sec:airpollutants} show why retaining the nitrogen pathways matters: the common ledger includes NO$_x$ removed during management and grazing alongside soil and burning sources, while distinct product factors drive fertiliser NH$_3$. External comparisons assess matched source boundaries and identify shared inputs rather than selecting a factor to match an external total.
