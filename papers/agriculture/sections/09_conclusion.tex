\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ===========================================================================

We have presented a reproducible system that computes agricultural greenhouse gases
and air pollutants for Pakistan from one shared activity and nitrogen accounting, and
distributes both to provinces, a \SI{0.05}{\degree} grid and 116 districts under a
reconciliation invariant. For 2024 it estimates \SI{223}{}~Mt~\COe{} and, in native
mass, \SI{2.7}{}~Mt \NHthree{} with \SI{0.65}{}~Mt CO and \SI{54}{}~kt PM$_{2.5}$ from
crop-residue burning. The ammonia figure is the estimate under the
manure-management configuration the greenhouse-gas inventory also uses; across
defensible alternative configurations it spans \SI{1.67}{}--\SI{2.67}{}~Mt, and that
structural range, rather than the parametric interval, is the honest measure of how
well the national total is constrained.

Two results seem to us to generalise beyond Pakistan.

The first is that \emph{agreement in aggregate does not imply agreement in the
components}, and this recurred at two scales. Putting the IPCC and EMEP/EEA nitrogen
chains on common boundaries made their livestock totals differ by only
\num{1.24}$\times$, but no individual stage agreed: field application and grazing
disagreed by factors of roughly two and three in opposite directions and partly
cancelled. The same pattern appeared in the spatial allocation, where two large proxy
errors had offset almost exactly. An inventory that checks only its totals will not
see either.

The second is that \emph{spatial pattern and emission magnitude can be separately
right or wrong}. Compared against an independent gridded ammonia inventory at its own
year, our surface agrees well on pattern while sitting \num{1.8} times higher in
level, by a near-constant factor across provinces. Reported per stream, and setting
aside the livestock comparison where both inventories distribute on the same livestock
product, the synthetic-fertiliser stream agrees on pattern \emph{and} level, while the
burning stream agrees on neither. A single national ratio would have hidden all of
this, and so would a single correlation coefficient.

What remains open is specific rather than diffuse. The manure-management partition is
not measured for Pakistan, the two external parameterisations that bear on it disagree
by a factor of three, and it dominates both the ammonia estimate and the manure
nitrous oxide. A national manure-management survey is the single highest-value data
collection this work points to. Beyond it, the burning geography needs an evaluation
against something other than the fire product used to allocate it. A monthly layer for
the sector now ships with the national release
(\texttt{outputs/agriculture\_monthly\_2024.csv}, and as this sector's slice of the
nine-year \texttt{outputs/pakemis\_monthly\_fy2015\_16\_fy2023\_24.csv}), and by the
release's own
declaration---\SI{84}{\percent} of the sector's emissions ride a
flat, declared profile. That figure is true and, read alone, misleading: most of the
flatness is correct rather than missing, because enteric fermentation and manure management
are continuous processes riding a census stock and no monthly acquisition could improve a
flat month there. Separating the two readings
(\texttt{outputs/agriculture\_timing\_classes.csv}) leaves
\SI{6.67}{\percent} of the nine-year panel, and \SI{7.00}{\percent} of the reference
year, whose timing this inventory should resolve and has not---almost all of it managed
soils. That, rather than the flat share, is the remaining temporal work. An exploratory land-sector extension is
provided only as Supplementary Note~S1 and is excluded from all agricultural results.

The design choice underlying all of it --- treating the national inventory as a control
value to which every spatial product must reconcile, while keeping emission factors and
spatial proxies as separable, auditable components --- resolves the usual tension
between spatial detail and reported figures. The result is a backbone a national team
can run, audit and update from a documented pipeline, and one that states plainly which
of its numbers are settled and which are not.

That design generalises past agriculture. The same control-total discipline now carries
nine further sectors on the same engine, and the reconciliation it enforces within this
sector is enforced between sectors too: the dung-cake nitrogen this inventory removes from
the manure-management balance is accounted for on the receiving side through
the same herd and the same volatile-solids rates---the part the household survey observes
as household fuel in the residential sector, the remainder as an unresolved row outside the
total---and the crop residue diverted to household
fuel (\SI{9.27}{}~Mt dry, \SI{122192}{}~TJ) is received by the same fuel ledger from the
residue balance, so neither pair can drift apart
\citep{ahmad2026national}. Agriculture is \SI{50.2}{\percent} of that national total and the
largest contributor to its uncertainty, which is why the manure-management survey named above
is a national priority and not only an agricultural one.
