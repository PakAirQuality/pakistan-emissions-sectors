\section{Conditional uncertainty and distribution shape}

For reported value $x$, proportional lower and upper ranges $l$ and $h$, and
$u\sim\mathcal{U}(0,1)$, the bounded split-triangular multiplier is
\begin{equation}
m(u)=\begin{cases}
1-l+l\sqrt{2u}, & u<0.5,\\
1+h-h\sqrt{2(1-u)}, & u\geq0.5.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
The reported input is the mode and median. Gas production, gathering and processing share the
production-activity draw; additional processing-coverage residuals are independent. Oil
production and its transport fallback share the oil draw. Gases belonging to one physical factor
group share a quantile transformed through their gas-specific bounds. All simulations use
10,000 iterations and seed 42.

\section{Official-inventory and computational audit}

The CRT audit preserves the source ZIP and workbook hashes, worksheet names, cell coordinates,
raw values, units, formulas and notation keys. Generated tables reproduce each numeric
activity--factor row in native gases, convert the submitted total to the common GWP basis and
close every step of the CRT-to-reconstruction waterfall. The complete records are archived in
\path{outputs/fugitives_unfccc_crt_cell_audit.csv},
\path{outputs/fugitives_unfccc_crt_reproduction.csv} and
\path{outputs/fugitives_unfccc_crt_waterfall_2022.csv}.

Automated QA covers factor units, registered activity years, source-row and segment closure,
pipeline and gas-flow identities, all 280 structural configurations, conditional interval
ordering, field-supply reconciliation, observation-match completeness, permutation
reproducibility, provincial reconciliation and manuscript-output agreement. The run manifest and
machine-readable result are stored in \path{outputs/fugitives_run_manifest_2024.json} and
\path{outputs/fugitives_qa_2024.csv}.
