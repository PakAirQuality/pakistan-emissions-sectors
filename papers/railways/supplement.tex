\section{The CRT cell audit}

The extraction re-derives every value from the archived workbook
(\texttt{PAK\_CRT\_2024.zip}, SHA-256 pinned by test) on each run, rows located by label
so a workbook revision breaks loudly. The category row books \num{56609.03} TJ (NCV
basis), \num{4194.75} kt \COtwo{}, \num{0.2208} kt \CHfour{} and \num{0.2207} kt
\NtwoO{}; the liquid-fuels row's implied-factor cells read \num{74.10} t \COtwo{}/TJ,
\num{3.90} kg \CHfour{}/TJ and \num{3.8995} kg \NtwoO{}/TJ. The full cell-by-cell listing,
with workbook coordinates, is \texttt{railways\_unfccc\_crt\_cell\_audit\_2024.csv}.

\section{The uncertainty inputs register}

Every quantity the uncertainty statement rests on is a row of
\texttt{railways\_uncertainty\_inputs\_2024.csv}: parametric rows (fuel quantities,
density, calorific values, gas and air factors, the BC fraction) each with source,
distribution and correlation group; structural rows (the factor-table choice, the 1A3c
boundary, consumed-equals-burnt timing, the sulfur case) explicitly \emph{not sampled};
and one row marked \textsc{retired}---the summary-vs-detail diesel conflict, settled by
the 2024--25 edition's restatement---kept so its absence from the scenario table reads as
resolution rather than omission.
