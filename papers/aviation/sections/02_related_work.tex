\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

IPCC Volume~2 Chapter~3 describes fuel- and movement-based aviation methods and requires the
domestic/international split to follow departure and landing locations, not airline nationality
\citep{ipcc2006energy}. International aviation is excluded from the national total and reported
as a memo item. These rules make fuel uplift a control, not a ready-made inventory, whenever
sales categories describe customers rather than flight stages.

The OECD air-transport database estimates monthly domestic, international, territory- and
residence-based \COtwo{} from ICAO flight data \citep{oecdaviation}. It supplies precisely the
accounting bridge needed here, but is not independent once its resident-carrier split is used
to allocate fuel. Climate TRACE supplies a second observation-informed estimate
\citep{climatetrace}; possible shared flight-surveillance inputs are reported rather than
ignored.

Aircraft-performance detail does not by itself resolve an accounting boundary. ICAO's Carbon
Emissions Calculator provides route- and aircraft-class fuel relationships intended for
passenger carbon estimation \citep{icaoicec2024}. OpenAP provides open aircraft properties,
kinematic and dynamic performance, fuel-flow models and reusable software
\citep{sun2020openap}. OpenSky provides research access to large-scale ADS-B observations but
coverage is spatially and temporally uneven \citep{schaefer2014opensky}. ADSB.lol also publishes
free daily readsb trace archives under the ODbL \citep{adsblolhistory,readsbjson}. We use the
first two for a provisional resolution experiment, execute a four-day public-data coverage
pilot with the latter and retain a common import contract for all observed providers; no
synthetic stage is presented as an observation.

For airport pollutants, ICAO Doc~9889 recommends actual aircraft--engine combinations and
operation-specific times in mode where available \citep{icao9889}. The ICAO Aircraft Engine
Emissions Databank supplies certified engine fuel flow and gaseous-emission measurements
\citep{icaoeedb}. This study uses representative engine fits as an explicit intermediate tier;
registration-level matching remains outside the central reconciliation study.
