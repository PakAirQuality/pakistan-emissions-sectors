\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
Station fuel consumption is stronger evidence than a generation-derived estimate, but coal origin, moisture and consignment NCV are not measured comprehensively. Default rank NCVs and carbon factors remain conditional parameters. National receipts, stock changes and consumption are distinct quantities; unresolved differences are not automatically assigned to a consumer.

The source convention for aggregate non-coal energy and gas gross-to-net conversion is imperfectly documented. K-Electric membership remains fuel-specific where compatible controls cannot select a unique boundary. The selected reference and a separate coverage alternative expose the consequence rather than adding both.

Captive gas is one supported fuel/use. Other captive fuels and the industrial engine/turbine partition remain incompletely observed. The national industrial account supplies other admitted fuel uses; the difference from a global inventory is not a floor on missing combustion.

Central power air coverage is deliberately narrow: oil/diesel \SOtwo{} only. Coal sulfur/ash analyses and demonstrated annual control operation are missing. Licence-application FGD and ESP values describe design, not verified performance. Screening endpoints are not bounds and are not included in conditional central percentiles. Particle size and filterable/condensable definitions limit cross-source comparisons.

Early fuel profiles and newer-year geography can be reconstructed or carried proxies. Facility status, source-period evidence and unallocated mass are preserved. Monthly grids represent allocated source mass; they do not supply vertical injection, detailed chemical speciation or a fully evaluated atmospheric-model input.
