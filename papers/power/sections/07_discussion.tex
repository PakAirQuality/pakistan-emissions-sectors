\section{Interpretation}
\label{sec:discussion}
The contribution is the reconciliation of physical station fuel, coal rank, generation and reporting boundaries. A single national energy column hides the rank and calorific assumptions that matter to coal tonnes. Retaining these quantities separately permits source-specific GHG and air methods and shows which differences arise from activity, factors, controls or coverage.

The selected coal reference is defensible without proving that its default NCVs are measured for every consignment. Likewise, public plant design evidence improves control scenarios without establishing actual annual stack removal. More detailed grid cells do not resolve those source uncertainties. The central air estimate and the separate coal/gas scenarios should be used for their stated purposes and never combined silently.

Captive self-generation belongs to industrial combustion. Publishing it in a power calculation module is an implementation convenience, while the category account prevents double counting. The fuel-specific K-Electric comparison illustrates why a single blanket coverage assumption would obscure important source boundaries.
