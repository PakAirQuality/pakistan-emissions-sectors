\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}
The maintained power inventory uses station coal consumption and rank-specific NCVs, preserves aggregate controls as explicit alternatives, and distinguishes public power from captive industrial fuel. The supported annual core reaches June 2025. Native GHG masses, GWP aggregates and air-pollutant masses retain their distinct conventions.

Reviewed oil/diesel \SOtwo{} enters the central air panel. Coal and other power pollutant/control cases remain separate because plant design does not demonstrate operation and fuel quality is incompletely observed. The release provides a source-specific basis for reviewing those limitations without hiding them in an uncontrolled endpoint or an undifferentiated national total.
