\section{Air-pollutant estimates and separate control scenarios}
\label{sec:air}
The central national air panel admits oil/diesel \SOtwo{} from a reviewed plant--fuel--year register. It contributes \SI{\SectorRefSOtwo}{\kilo\tonne} in FY2023--24 and \SI{\SectorLatestSOtwo}{\kilo\tonne} in FY2024--25. Fuel mass, equipment-specific factor applicability, sulfur basis, assumed removal and reporting boundary are recorded separately. The FY2024--25 admitted RFO quantity is 95.11 kt against a compatible 102.6 kt national control. K-Electric remains a separate coverage case.

Coal, gas and bagasse pollutant scenarios do not enter those central totals. AP-42 bituminous/lignite \SOtwo{} factors are $38S$/$30S$ lb per short ton, with sulfur in weight percent. Filterable particulate factors are $10A$/$6.5A$; size fractions use the applicable source tables. Rank fuel energy comes from the same physical coal account as GHG. Thar sulfur spans 0.26--2.90\%; this range remains an explicit imported-coal proxy where no consignment analysis is held. It is not a measured imported composition or a probability distribution.

Public generation licence applications provide plant-specific \emph{design} evidence. Port Qasim's application (PDF page 69) specifies limestone--gypsum wet FGD at no less than 92\%, five-field ESP bulk-ash removal of 99.7\%, low-NOx burners and planned monitoring. CIHC/China Power Hub's application (PDF page 204) specifies seawater FGD at no less than 93\%. These documents do not establish actual annual removal, bypass or availability. Bulk ESP efficiency is not transferred to PM$_{2.5}$.

The separate selected-plant \SOtwo{} cases apply the design FGD efficiency once to station coal consumption and an explicit sulfur proxy. The uncontrolled and design-operation results are conditional scenarios, not central releases, mathematical maxima/minima or 95\% confidence limits. Generic controlled coal/gas scenarios remain technology transfers where plant-specific operation is unresolved. Captive gas is counted once in industrial air emissions.

Monthly timing for admitted stations is labelled a parent-fuel-fleet proxy when only aggregate monthly fuel is available. The admitted stations use their own spatial frame. A national fleet profile is not observed timing or geography for a subset.
