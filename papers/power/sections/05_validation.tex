\section{Source controls and evaluation}
\label{sec:validation}
\subsection{Fuel and generation controls}\label{sec:heatrate}\label{sec:impliedcv}
The final station ledger is checked for unique plant--fuel--year records and compatible units. Plant generation sums are compared with separately read system totals. Fuel mass, NCV-derived heat and electricity imply an efficiency diagnostic, with rank-specific heating values retained. An incompatible publisher coal-energy control is exposed rather than reconciled by changing station consumption. Physical station fuel and national aggregate energy are separate observations with different uncertainty and boundary support.

\subsection{National and external comparisons}\label{sec:crt}\label{sec:trace}
National reporting, EDGAR and Climate TRACE are compared only at matched periods, source scope and gas conventions. Shared energy-balance inputs reduce independence. Agreement with a filing compiled from the same balance cannot validate the disputed calorific conversion, and a difference from EDGAR does not measure missing fuel.

\subsection{K-Electric membership}\label{sec:keleg}
CPPA/GENCO, K-Electric and national fuel totals are compared for gas, oil and coal separately. Membership inferred for one fuel is not imposed on the others. The FY2024--25 K-Electric gas/RLNG volumes are converted from the reported gross-heat basis to the same net calorific basis used for station combustion before calculating either the coverage ratio or the additional-KE alternative. Oil and coal retain their own physical fuel bases. The supported reference boundary and additional-KE alternative remain separate, preventing duplicated electricity or fuel.

\subsection{Satellite evidence}\label{sec:tropomi}
Held satellite products may describe regional and seasonal patterns near facilities. They are column retrievals affected by transport, chemistry, cloud sampling and retrieval sensitivity. A concentration--emission correlation alone is not an emission validation, and the reported fuel/control scenarios are not tuned to satellite columns. A plume inversion or atmospheric-model comparison is outside the present inventory's completion scope.
