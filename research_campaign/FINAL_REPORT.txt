A verification campaign over a frozen release: close accounting, uncertainty and

THE SEVEN FINDINGS THAT MATTER
------------------------------
1  THE FOREST FLUX DISAGREES IN DIRECTION, so no land component can be built without
   choosing a side. Pakistan's own submission prints forest land at +28.44 Mt CO2 as a
   SOURCE; FAOSTAT books the same year at -4.107 as a SINK; the gap is 32.55 Mt, about 7%
   of the national total. The mechanism is in FAOSTAT's own columns - forest area rising at
   exactly +20.786 kha/yr for four consecutive years is interpolation, so the only annual
   series carries no annual information, and net forest conversion is exactly 0.000 kha
   from 2020.
   AND THE DECISIVE PART IS ABOUT THIS PROJECT: its exploratory land module's -3.52 Mt sink
   is FAOSTAT's zero read a second time, and the justification written beside that
   parameter - "afforestation offsets gross loss" - is contradicted by the national CRT,
   where gross losses are 2.09x gross gains.

2  A VERSION STRING IS NOT A PIN. Two internally consistent artefacts both labelled
   1.0.0rc59 carry different content: the served copy is byte-identical to commit 5915e0c0
   and this tree has moved past it. All nine national totals agree to NINE DECIMAL PLACES
   while the provincial allocation differs - a declared furnace-oil re-base that landed
   after the label was published.
   Nothing could see it, because every guard this project has on a release's numbers is a
   guard on a PERIOD TOTAL. The same blind spot then turned up on the time axis in the
   national monthly table. Both axes are now fingerprinted per table per vintage, with
   fact_annual and the air layer as controls.

3  A PROFILE SERVED AS "OBSERVED" PUTS 78.76 PERCENT OF A YEAR IN ONE MONTH. The crude-oil
   timing for FY2021-22 is Balochistan's record alone, because the coverage test asks for
   twelve months rather than for four provinces. PBS prints all four; the parse loses two,
   because the mineral's name cell is vertically centred across its five province rows and
   a layout extraction puts the name on the middle line. The identity proves the
   attribution to the unit - the four provinces equal the printed total in EVERY one of the
   twelve months, 9,678,139 both sides for FY2021-22 coal - while the panel carries
   4,072,263, or 42.1%.
   AND THE PARSE'S OWN CONTROL CANNOT SEE IT: it compares the rows the parser buffered
   against the printed total, not the rows the panel ships, so it closes exactly while
   certifying a quantity the output does not contain. Nine canonical mineral-years disagree
   between a CLOSING control and the shipped panel, from 6.9% to 230.8%.

4  THE DEPOSIT OFFERED 71 ODbL ROWS UNDER CC-BY. Both facility registries carry a licence
   column marking 71 of 12,680 rows as OpenStreetMap derivatives, and both shipped as
   own-work - the exact claim the limitations section says this project cannot make for
   anything it does not own. REPAIRED: they ship as mixed-per-row with the per-row counts
   in the reason, and the builder fails closed on a stale declaration. The seven whole-file
   carve-outs are now named by published identity AND by the path the archive ships them
   at, because the two differ after the data-layout migration and a reader searching for
   the first found nothing.

5  THE F-GAS LEAK RATE DECIDES WHEN, NOT WHETHER. The bank's single refrigeration rate lies
   outside two of the five IPCC Table 7.9 bands it lumps; across the band envelope the
   operating channel moves 155x (0.01329 to 2.06204 Mt) while the stratum total moves 2.9x,
   because with no recovery credited the gas not leaked in operation is emitted at
   retirement. Pakistan's own 2.F filing is one species, HFC-23, at exactly 1% of a
   declared 23.6954857 t stock, filed under commercial refrigeration - where HFC-23 is not
   used. Its first BTR then books 2.F, 2.C, 2.E and 2.G all at zero, so the two most recent
   official submissions disagree about whether the category exists.

6  CITATIONS MEASURED, AND "POINTED" SPLIT FROM "CHECKABLE". Of 122 registered sources
   where a table pointer is the right notion, 104 carry one and 18 do not - against a
   register-only scan that had reported 157 missing by ignoring the per-row citations the
   ingests carry. Of the 104, 94 point into a document this repository archives, 6 into
   documents it does not hold and 4 into derived files. OCAC (7), PBS minerals (3) and the
   UNFCCC rows were closed by reading documents already held; five pre-existing pointers
   were checked to the cell and all five held. The 18 is deliberately conservative: a few
   dataset sources would move to "not applicable" if the audit's publisher markers were
   widened, which would improve the share without citing anything.

7  CLEANING A PAPER DIRECTORY DISARMS THE GUARD THAT READS THE COMPILER'S LOGS. The only
   check that can see a table running off the page reads pdftex's logs, which are build
   artefacts: with them deleted it read nothing, asserted an empty set and PASSED. Found by
   doing it, and fixed with the positive control it never had.

WHAT WAS VERIFIED AND FOUND REDUNDANT
-------------------------------------
Two delivered worker lanes were checked claim by claim. Both were CORRECT and almost
entirely REDUNDANT - the F-gas extraction, the comparator lanes, the BTR-zero tier and all
four overlap claims were already in the repository. Their value was an independent second
reading, which is worth something and is not the same as a finding. Three things they
reported did need action: a bunkering absence argued from "Pakistan had no formal bunkering
market until 2026" while the held BTR books 0.040 Mt (repaired in all four places it was
load-bearing); a comparator registered as partially independent where 29 of 52 coordinates
are measurably derived from it; and a comparator applying ONE intensity, 2431 g CO2e/kWh,
to every Pakistani coal asset. One lane finding was simply wrong: Lakhra's three units are
mothballed, not operating, so the coverage hole it reported does not exist.

ELEVEN DEFECTS WERE MINE
------------------------
Recorded in full in DECISIONS.txt, because the pattern is the point: every one was caught
by a guard or control written before the result was trusted. A detector that invented
matches. A check that could not hold in the state it ran in, four times. A scanner blind to
a notation, which had left three printed numbers unguarded for as long as that notation has
existed. An anti-shadow guard that refused a convenient fix and forced two unverifiable
magnitudes out of the paper. A builder that could not overwrite its own output. A reference
checker wrong three ways before it was right. A claim table that named commands which did
not reproduce its numbers. And one framing corrected outright: D072 implied the minerals
provinces were missing from the source; D075 proved they are printed and the parse loses
them, which is worse.

WHAT REMAINS, AND WHO DECIDES IT
--------------------------------
FIVE DECISIONS FOR THE USER, each of which moves a published product or a pin:
  - the PBS minerals parse: attribute province rows preceding a name line to that mineral.
    Specified; moves the monthly shapes of at least nine canonical mineral-years.
  - covers() requiring the country rather than twelve months: ten mineral-years would fall
    from T2 observed to T4 days-weighted.
  - the F-gas refrigeration leak rate, pending a domestic/commercial split nobody holds.
  - the exploratory LULUCF deforestation parameter, which would move a published figure.
  - a per-sector binding for the coal CH4 shared group in parameter_scope.csv - one line,
    but that file drives the uncertainty engine's correlation structure.
  And a sixth that is release mechanics rather than science: 1.0.0rc59 has been published
  from a different tree, so the next mint must bump the version.
FOR THE AUTHOR OR AN EDITOR: the ESSD AI policy; a repository-native deposit and a DOI;
  and the submission decision on ODbL-derived tables in a primary CC-BY record.
NEEDS AN ACQUISITION, all confirmed unheld: roundwood for harvested wood products,
  paraffin wax, SF6 nameplate charge, pre-charged equipment imports, port bunkering records.

HOW TO READ THIS CAMPAIGN
-------------------------
It moved no number, and that was the point. What it changed is what the release can be
shown to support: a label that no longer identifies its content is a row rather than a
discovery; a control that certifies a quantity its output does not contain is measured
rather than trusted; an archive that offered 71 share-alike rows under the wrong grant says
what it ships; and a guard that passes on an unbuilt tree now fails and says so. Six
