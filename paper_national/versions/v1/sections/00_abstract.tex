PakEmis reconstructs Pakistan's non-LULUCF greenhouse-gas and air-pollutant emissions from a shared national activity and material-flow account. Release \PakEmisRelease\ covers ten fiscal years, July 2015--June 2025, with a separate source-limited provisional FY2025--26 extension. This technical report supports the national data-description article and maintained sector notes.

The S\_TAN reference produces \SI{\PakRefARfive}{\mega\tonne} CO$_2$-equivalent in FY2023--24 and \SI{\PakLatestARfive}{\mega\tonne} in FY2024--25 on AR5 GWP-100. Native CO$_2$, CH$_4$ and N$_2$O remain separately reported. Reference-year covered emissions include \SI{\PakRefPMfine}{\giga\gram} primary PM$_{2.5}$, \SI{\PakRefNOx}{\giga\gram} NO$_2$-equivalent NO$_x$ and \SI{\PakRefNHthree}{\giga\gram} NH$_3$. Seven core air species have substantive source methods, results and evaluation; additional particulate strata retain narrower coverage.

Station coal consumption and rank-specific NCVs, reconciled consumer accounts, survey-supported household fuels and conserved nitrogen pathways distinguish this reconstruction from a clipped global inventory. Shared conditional realisations produce native/GWP levels and paired trend intervals, with structural allocations, control scenarios and missing sources kept separate. Monthly and geographic products retain process-specific support, unallocated mass and licensing boundaries. Exact-period public HTAP/EDGAR comparisons identify shared inputs and important source discrepancies. The locally prepared review package includes data, code, metadata, figures, reproduction instructions and checksums; external review deposit and a persistent identifier remain pending.
