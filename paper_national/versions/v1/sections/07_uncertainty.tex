\section{Conditional uncertainty and structural alternatives}\label{sec:uncertainty}
\subsection{Parameter ensembles}\label{sec:paramcoverage}\label{sec:ensemble}
The annual ensembles use 10\,000 common parameter realisations with recorded seed and named dependence setting. Positive activity/factor multipliers use their declared distributions; physical fractions and native fate parameters retain bounds and material constraints. Shared activity is represented by actual common draws rather than by summing independent sector output errors.

Nitrogen replays native NH$_3$, NO$_x$ and N$_2$O from the same herd, crops, nutrient inputs and surviving state. Synthetic fertiliser product factors share their applicable input realisations with indirect N$_2$O. Waste fate regenerates the burned dry mass supplied to air factors, without resampling population or collection a second time. Fugitive activity/control realisations remain distinct from unrelated infrastructure measures. Ledger-L household dung uses the same sampled herd as manure accounting. Coal rank heat and carbon factors preserve the station reference.

Remaining source factor/technology assumptions are conditional where their dependence or performance is not observed. Equipment and manure-system shares are held rather than independently multiplied outside their partition. Particulate containment is checked at matched central source/process boundaries; the marginal factor transfer model does not constitute a measured joint particulate composition distribution. Sulfur policy/specification and plant operating alternatives are structural evidence, not sampling distributions merely because endpoints are available.

The reference national AR5 parameter interval is \SIrange{\GHGReferenceLow}{\GHGReferenceHigh}{\mega\tonne} CO$_2$-equivalent.\fi
\ifcsname PakRefPMfineLow\endcsname
Covered air intervals are \SIrange{\PakRefPMfineLow}{\PakRefPMfineHigh}{\giga\gram} PM$_{2.5}$, \SIrange{\PakRefNOxLow}{\PakRefNOxHigh}{\giga\gram} NO$_x$ and \SIrange{\PakRefNHthreeLow}{\PakRefNHthreeHigh}{\giga\gram} NH$_3$.\fi
These are conditional parameter percentiles under S\_TAN and persistent parameters. Missing categories, structural coverage/control choices and temporal/spatial allocation uncertainty are not incorporated.

\subsection{Paired trends}\label{sec:longitudinal}
Each change is calculated within a paired realisation before percentiles are taken. Persistent-parameter and annual-activity-error cases test unidentified temporal dependence. Subtracting unrelated annual percentiles does not produce a trend interval. Source revisions, maturity and coverage are retained in the interpretation, so a partial FY2026 sum never enters a common national change.

\subsection{Structural cases}\label{sec:bridge}
The four integrated configurations are S\_TAN, L\_TAN, S\_IPCC and L\_IPCC. Household S/L and nitrogen formulations are separate structural axes and are applied consistently to both product families. Other source cases include coal allocation/operating season, sulfur evidence, transferred road factors, household technology/fuel choices, waste burning fraction and regional fertiliser factors. Scenario values have no assigned probabilities and their extrema are not 95\% limits.

Dated kiln-fleet cases keep observed dates, fleet coverage and season assumptions. Cases with a negative supply-minus-use quantity expose incompatibility rather than force closure; they are not mixed into an interval simply to cover an external total. Selected-plant FGD design cases apply an assumed efficiency once, without claiming demonstrated operation or an upper bound.

\subsection{Dominant uncertainty and reporting}\label{sec:priorities}
Pollutant-specific driver records distinguish incomplete-combustion factors and household/waste activity from nitrogen and sulfur inputs. Correlation sensitivity diagnostics are not additive variance shares. Managed-soil factor applicability remains separate for irrigated cropland, rice and grazing; agreement with FAOSTAT does not select the reference factor. Unestimated sources and allocation uncertainty remain explicit limitations even where a parameter interval is relatively narrow.
