\section{Source importance and uncertainty priorities}\label{sec:keycat}
Source contributions are evaluated within each native species or declared GWP metric. Agriculture dominates much of the methane/nitrogen account, while household fuel and open burning contribute substantial primary particulate. Industrial sulfur and combustion NO$_x$ do not follow the same ordering. BC/OC and particulate strata are not summed to make a combined pollutant rank.

Source importance combines supported mass, plausible uncertainty and coverage. A small conditional percentile interval does not make an unestimated category negligible. The consequential scientific uncertainties include manure shares and soil-factor applicability for nitrogen, household dung destination and combustion factors for particulate, coal consumer allocation and fuel/control evidence for sulfur, and transferred road technology/class assumptions for NO$_x$.

Key-category and driver products retain the declared source/category/metric boundaries. Driver correlations are sensitivities rather than additive attribution. The main contribution is tracing which activity, factor, control, coverage or allocation can explain an important difference, rather than listing a large number of small factors or ranking screening endpoints alongside central estimates.
