\section{Reproduction and release record}\label{app:repro}
The release manifest identifies exact version/commit, configurations, periods, product metadata and checksums. Reproduction uses the documented dependency environment and retained inputs. A compiled-input build regenerates products from canonical activity tables; primary-source re-extraction additionally requires the retained document bytes and source parsers. The verification record states exactly which level succeeded and which inputs or primary-source steps were not executed.

A clean environment checks dependency compatibility and performs source/export, material/category, species-unit/non-additivity, annual/monthly, spatial, period/configuration, uncertainty/trend and query/download verification. Numerical tolerances reflect source precision and stored float32 cell values; a meaningful discrepancy is not rounded away. Repaired errors include final-exported regression checks rather than tests only of parser accumulators.

\section{Monthly evidence}\label{app:monthly}
Monthly profiles distinguish own-source observations, compatible reconstructed activity, process proxies and declared unsupported/flat seasonality. Final source controls determine evidence admission. Fertiliser offtake, crop calendars, climate-based household profiles, sales and fire observations represent different quantities. Newly extended annual data do not imply newly observed monthly release dates. Temporal evidence shares are species-specific and accompany source profiles.

\section{Geographic evidence}\label{app:spatial}
Spatial records distinguish facility points, administrative controls, own-year support, fixed surfaces, reference patterns and substitute proxies. The reporting mask identifies territory/statistical boundary differences. Placed plus unallocated mass is tested by species/configuration/year. Supported fine-scale geography can be absent even when provincial activity is known. Within-year monthly geography is held where no source movement is observed.

Primary geometry excludes ODbL-derived road/rail/coordinate effects at calculation time; removing files from an archive alone would not remove their derived effect. Independently supported geography and explicit unallocated/accounting alternatives are retained. Preserved ODbL products carry their separate obligations and do not receive a blanket CC BY label.

\section{Sources and immutable review assets}\label{app:sources}
The source register, availability table, retrieval hashes and controls identify official editions and exact periods. Products retain their original evidence and units; source documents are redistributed only where terms support it. The local immutable review package contains code, supported data, metadata, figures/plotted data, manuscript files, licences, reproduction instructions and checksums. The remaining external repository deposit/submission action is not reported as completed, and no DOI or review link is invented.
