\section{Coverage and notation statuses}
\label{sec:completeness}
The inventory remains non-LULUCF. Supported CO$_2$, CH$_4$ and N$_2$O are reported as native gas masses, with GWP aggregates separately. The core air panel comprises primary PM$_{2.5}$, BC, NO$_x$, SO$_2$, CO, NMVOC and NH$_3$. PM$_{10}$, TSP and OC retain their narrower source coverage. Estimated module cells do not establish a complete national source account.

\subsection{Supported processes and material gaps}
Industrial combustion includes admitted gas, liquids, cement/kiln coal, brick firing and captive gas. The coal account distinguishes physical station power, industrial uses and unresolved quantities. An external industrial total minus this estimate is a signed comparison, not a lower or upper bound on missing emissions. Source revisions and allocation choices can change that difference without changing source coverage.

IPPU includes cement process CO$_2$ and supported ammonia, steel and soda-ash process estimates at their admitted boundaries. A soda-ash category is not NA merely because an earlier release lacked a method. Fertiliser product tonnage is not ammonia output, and product/output, feedstock carbon and recovered CO$_2$ remain separate. Nitric acid, lime, glass, urea-finishing air releases and other unresolved industrial sources retain NE statuses where their physical process/control evidence is insufficient.

F-gases, coal-mining fugitive coverage and remaining waste-treatment sources are screened separately where supported. No global inventory estimate is added to the central total to close a missing category. Commercial/institutional fuel use and domestic navigation retain their documented boundaries or omissions. LULUCF exploratory material is excluded from every national core total and comparison.

Power air admission covers reviewed oil/diesel SO$_2$. Coal, gas and bagasse pollutant/control cases remain separate screening or design scenarios. Public licences establish proposed equipment at selected coal stations but not annual operation. Other central power species therefore remain NE. Industry, IPPU and fugitives have admitted provisional air estimates; they are not absent simply because older aggregate descriptions omitted them.

Road air includes Tier-1 hot/cold exhaust, evaporative NMVOC, tyre/brake and road-surface wear. Off-road machinery and resuspended road dust are separate missing air estimates. Household fossil and biomass emissions are included at the gross appliance boundary; generators remain an air gap. Agricultural NH$_3$ and NO$_x$ use the shared nitrogen pathways, while machinery and tillage/harvest dust remain missing sources. Waste burning is estimated with origin-based geography; treatment/incineration estimates are not manufactured from waste generation.

\subsection{Transfers and non-additive quantities}
Household electricity is counted where generation occurs. Captive self-generation reports under industrial combustion. Diverted dung and crop residues leave their source ledgers once; reference S receives observed household fuel, and unresolved dung destinations are not automatically burned. L is an explicit alternative destination assumption. Biogenic CO$_2$ remains a memo item under its documented convention.

BC and OC are components of PM$_{2.5}$, and PM$_{2.5}$ is contained in compatible PM$_{10}$/TSP. Their masses are not added across nested species. NO$_x$ is NO$_2$-equivalent and NH$_3$ is molecular NH$_3$, distinct from intermediate nitrogen masses. No ozone or total ambient particulate is estimated by summing precursor emissions.
