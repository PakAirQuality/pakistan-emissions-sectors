% ===========================================================================
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% ===========================================================================

We have presented a ten-sector national greenhouse-gas inventory for Pakistan totalling
\SI{444.96}{\mega\tonne} \COe{} on AR5 under reference scenario \texttt{REF\_2023\_24}, built as a modular software system in which each
sector satisfies a common contract enforced by \num{32} invariants, and in which the
properties that span sectors are executable assertions rather than statements in a methods
document.

Three results generalise beyond this country.

Accounting boundaries can be closed by construction. Four quantities that are conventionally
estimated twice and reconciled by reading are here computed once and consumed twice, with
equality asserted exactly: two are transfers between modules, one closes a waste-fate
partition inside one module, and one partitions a transport total between its reported
categories. Each of the four existed first as a written-down known
defect---an orphaned nitrogen flow, a double-counted diesel, two unpartitioned totals---which
suggests that a well-documented limitation is a specification for the work that removes it.

The IPCC default tables are a systematic trap. In four of six fuel-burning sectors, the
plausible wrong table leaves \COtwo{} exactly right while the trace gases move: the
affected category by \SI{8.0}{\percent} in the two cases where it is not \COtwo{}-dominated,
and the \CHfour{} row several-fold in the two where it is. Either way the error is invisible
to every external validation available, because every comparator is \COtwo{}-dominated. We would
expect an audit of which table each fuel-using category actually drew from to be productive
across many published inventories.

An uncertainty architecture can manufacture its own findings. An earlier edition of this paper
reported that correlating shared emission factors widened the road-transport category from
\SI{11.96}{} to \SI{18.46}{\percent}, and read that as a property of the inventory. It was a
property of the model: a component could carry only one correlation group, so putting a row
into the fuel-carbon group moved its \emph{entire} variance with a factor that accounts for
about two points of it. Once each row's activity, conversion and factor are carried as separate
parameters, the same question leaves the national
uncertainty at $\pm\SI{11.72}{\percent}$ either way, and road transport's
at $\pm\SI{13.3}{\percent}$. We report both readings and choose
neither, as we do for the disputed sewer methane correction factor that moves domestic
wastewater by a factor of \num{1.6}. Both are conditional on the reference structural
configuration, and Section~\ref{sec:bridge} prices the named alternatives that no parametric
interval contains. The lesson is not that shared factors matter more than was thought; it is
that a dependency model too coarse to represent the question will answer it anyway.

For Pakistan specifically, the analysis produces one clear recommendation and one clear
constraint. The recommendation is to resolve gas-system technology and facility activity:
the 2019 segment ledger now includes utility throughput and pipelines, while material,
pressure, LDAR, compressor, processing and well evidence still determine its structural
range. The constraint is that
part of industrial fuel combustion is still outside the estimate. The solid-fuel
activation admitted the brick-kiln and cement-sector coal this sentence once called
absent, so what stands against the roughly
\SI{44}{\mega\tonne} EDGAR reports for 1A2 is a smaller and now unidentified remainder
rather than the non-gas fuels as a class, and no refinement of the estimated gas component
substitutes for what is still missing.

Everything here is \emph{computationally} reproducible from the committed activity tables,
and the qualifier is the honest part. The engine, the activity data, the emission factors
and every output file quoted are public; the test suite runs on every commit; each figure is
regenerated from the engine's own outputs and a test fails if any is stale; and the numbers
in this manuscript are checked against the generated CSVs they came from. What is \emph{not}
reproducible end-to-end is the step before that: \num{12} of \num{217} activity files cite
at least one document this project does not hold, so their derivation from the raw source
cannot be re-run by a reader in full, only their use downstream. The register names every one. Six of the
\num{217} are this project's own compiled fact tables---the fugitives anchor activity, the
two residential fuel-supply tables and the three IPPU company tables---which the sector data
layout registered as sources in their own right rather than leaving them implicit behind the
compatibility views generated from them. A seventh joined them with the power coal re-base:
the coal-energy booking table, which declares which account the engine books and is derived
from two products rather than transcribed from a document. The other \num{210} rows are
unchanged. An inventory whose
claims can be re-derived by a reader is a different kind of object from one that can only be
read, and building it that way cost less than the reconciliation work it replaced.
