\section{Dataset scope and architecture}
\label{sec:scope}

This section states what the dataset is and is not. PakEmis is a public-data
reconstruction of Pakistan's anthropogenic greenhouse-gas emissions outside land use,
land-use change and forestry, reported for fiscal years running from July to June and
carried on one reference configuration whose alternatives are registered rather than
implied. The architecture that produces it is described first, because the properties a
reader can rely on---mass conservation from category to gas to total, one reporting
period across every sector, and an explicit notation key wherever a category is not
estimated---are properties of that architecture and are asserted by it on every build.
The completeness statement that closes the section is the boundary of the estimated
scope: what is estimated, what is not, and what a comparator implies the absent part is
worth.
