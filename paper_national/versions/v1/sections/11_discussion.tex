\section{Interpretation and contribution}\label{sec:discussion}
The inventory's practical contribution is a Pakistan-specific source reconstruction that connects material and activity evidence to distinct GHG and air calculations. Station fuel and rank NCVs expose the public-power conversion choice. Industrial coal residuals and dated fleet alternatives expose allocation assumptions. Survey fuel distinguishes supported household combustion from other diverted material. Nitrogen stages explain linked NH$_3$, NO$_x$ and N$_2$O consequences. These examples provide information that a clipped global inventory cannot supply from a national cell sum.

The common account does not make all pollutants equally assured. Reviewed oil/diesel SO$_2$ is admitted separately from coal/gas/control scenarios; industry/IPPU/fugitives include provisional source estimates; road evaporation and wear coexist with missing resuspended dust and off-road air sources. Species-specific coverage and controls therefore matter to every national comparison.

Household primary PM$_{2.5}$, agricultural NH$_3$, industrial sulfur and combustion NO$_x$ describe different processes. There is no general inverse ordering of GHG and air rankings, and gross mass cannot rank exposure or health priorities. Missing vertical injection, chemical speciation and outdoor escape limits prevent an ambient-ready interpretation.

Annual extension and detailed reference choice are distinct. Later annual controls can support FY2025 magnitude without supporting new monthly release dates or finer source geography. The FY2026 provisional source products retain their exact periods and partial scope. Fixed location maps can distribute a changing national mass but do not establish changing local activity.

Shared conditional inputs and material closure improve coherence while leaving uncertainty in fate, controls, factor applicability and unestimated categories visible. Structural alternatives remain interpretable source cases without invented probabilities. The review package and consistent machine-readable records permit those decisions to be assessed and revised on evidence rather than hidden in one aggregate number.
