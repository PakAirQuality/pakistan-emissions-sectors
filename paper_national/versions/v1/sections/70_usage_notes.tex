\section{Usage notes and limitations}
\label{sec:usage}

What the dataset supports depends on the scale at which it is read. At national scale it is
a reconstruction of the estimated scope on a named reference configuration, with a
parametric interval and a priced set of structural alternatives; at monthly scale it is a
monthly estimate whose evidence class is stated per stream and is flat for a declared share
of the mass; at administrative and grid scale it is a spatial allocation on proxies and on
registered point sources, and must not be read as observed facility emissions. The
limitations below are labelled so that a claim elsewhere in the paper can be bounded by
one of them, and the discussion that follows them says what the construction bought and
what it did not.
