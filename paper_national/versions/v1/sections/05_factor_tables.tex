\section{Source factors and chemical conventions}\label{sec:factors}
Stationary-combustion factors are selected by reporting category and fuel. Public energy industries, manufacturing/captive generation and residential combustion use their applicable IPCC tables, even where one gas factor happens to be equal \citep{ipcc2006energy,ipcc2019}. Coal mass is converted with documented rank NCVs before per-energy factors are applied. A factor already incorporating oxidation or control is not discounted twice. Air factors retain the source-specific EMEP/EEA or AP-42 process boundary \citep{emep2019,epaap42}.

NH$_3$-N factors become molecular NH$_3$ by 17/14; NO-N becomes NO$_2$-equivalent by 46/14. Source NO factors receive 46/30 once. CO$_2$-equivalent is used only for declared GHG metrics. BC/OC are components of PM$_{2.5}$; compatible particulate strata are nested and are not summed.

AP-42 typical uncontrolled factors and transferred controlled-operation rows are scenarios where operation is unresolved. Plant design removal is distinct from installed and demonstrated annual performance. Power central admission is reviewed oil/diesel SO$_2$, while selected coal design cases remain separate. Fuel sulfur specimens, specification ceilings, raw-material sulfur, retention and removal have different evidence support.

The non-rice soil reference retains the dry-climate factor conditionally; irrigated cropland, flooded rice and grazing require separate applicability assessment. Published aggregate/climate alternatives remain structural, with no tuning to FAOSTAT. Factor registers preserve native unit, original particulate definition, source/table, technology, applicability, evidence and uncertainty representation.
