\section{Source data and processing}
\label{sec:sources}

Every activity quantity in the dataset is traced to a document in the public record: the
regulators' annual reports and the statistics bureau's yearbooks for energy, the economic
survey and the fertiliser development centre for agriculture, census and survey tables for
population and households, and the international defaults where the country publishes no
factor of its own. The source hierarchy---a national document over an international
default, a period-matched edition over a borrowed one, a transcribed table over an inferred
one---is applied per stream and recorded per stream, so that the evidence grade of a number
is a column beside it rather than a sentence about it. Two released products are the
register of record for this section and are described with the other data records in
Section~\ref{sec:records}: \texttt{outputs/pakemis\_source\_register.csv} names every source
document the release read, its hash, its custody status and where its publisher provides
it; \texttt{outputs/pakemis\_source\_streams.csv} joins each source to the emission streams
it feeds and carries the temporal and spatial evidence classes that the monthly estimate and
the spatial allocation inherit from it. Transcriptions are held to their documents by a
re-extraction check, and the transformations between a document's units and the
inventory's are code in the repository, not steps in a methods note. The redistribution
terms that follow from this custody model are stated in the Data availability section:
the derived products are open, and the source documents are named and located rather than
re-published.
