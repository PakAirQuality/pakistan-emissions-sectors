\section{Matched evaluation}\label{sec:benchmarks}
Internal source/export, material-flow, annual/monthly and placed/unallocated checks establish consistency. External evidence evaluates magnitude, change, timing or location at matched boundaries. The comparison records retain species, units, periods, provider and shared inputs; national agreement is not sufficient to select a factor or validate spatial placement.

\subsection{Magnitude and category boundaries}\label{sec:btr}
National reporting and EDGAR are compared by native gas, GWP convention and source category. Fiscal and calendar periods are retained explicitly. Energy-balance inputs shared with Pakistani statistics limit independence. A signed industrial difference is not missing emissions and can reflect revised coal allocation, calorific basis, controls or coverage.

Industrial air comparisons distinguish EDGAR fossil fuel from fossil-plus-biomass and include matched cement process emissions where the PakEmis integrated class requires that boundary. Other IPPU remains separate. The latest fully supported fiscal comparison is FY2021--22 because the air comparator ends in calendar 2022. Later PakEmis years are not compared using extrapolated external values. Large BC/CO differences motivate factor/equipment investigation rather than tuning the local account to the comparator.

HTAPv3.2 provides public annual/monthly country bundles without credentials. Original Pakistan NH$_3$ rows are reconciled against printed annual controls before exact July--June matching. Agriculture and road comparisons cover FY2015--16 through FY2019--20; later complete fiscal external values are absent because the source ends in calendar 2020. Household gross emissions and waste burning differ from the external broader boundaries and therefore receive contextual values, without a misleading ratio.

HTAP uses REAS regional baselines, EDGAR gap fills/agricultural burning and EDGAR post-2015 trends. Shared statistics and provider provenance limit independence; agreement with both HTAP and EDGAR is not two independent constraints. FY2019--20 S\_TAN agricultural NH$_3$ is approximately 2215 Gg against 2184 Gg in HTAP. Component pathways and factor applicability remain important even when aggregate magnitudes are close.

\subsection{Change and timing}
Overlapping source editions separate historical revisions, source additions and actual activity change. Monthly source rows and compatible national/annual controls are checked on the final exported panel. Uniform duplication can cancel in a normalised profile but still produces a false activity export; it is repaired at source. Correct extraction does not eliminate publication anomalies, which retain downgraded evidence and declared fallbacks.

Seasonal source evidence evaluates relevant emitting processes. Sales, crop calendars, fertiliser supply and fire detections are proxies with different physical meanings. An unsupported application or evaporation profile is not called observed solely because it spans twelve months.

\subsection{Location}\label{sec:benchmarks-provincial-so2}
Selected facilities, administrative activity and held-out district crop records test location. Inputs used to construct a proxy cannot independently validate that same proxy; opposing province differences may cancel nationally. Coarse or fixed surfaces do not observe local trend changes. Satellite NO$_2$, SO$_2$, NH$_3$, AOD and fire products require transport, chemistry, retrieval sensitivity and sampling treatment. No simple column/concentration--emission correlation is treated as validation, and source factors are not tuned solely to match an external pattern.
