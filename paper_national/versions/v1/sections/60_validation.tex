\section{Technical validation}
\label{sec:validation}

Validation here means two different things, and the section keeps them apart. The first
is internal: the annual, category and gas totals close exactly; every annual cell equals
the sum of its twelve months; the national total equals the spatial allocation plus its
unallocated remainder; and the ledgers between sectors balance. Those identities are
asserted on every build and are reported with the generation stages that produce them.
The second is external, and is the substance of this section: each sector is compared
against whichever independent estimates exist for it, the disagreements are explained or
defended rather than absorbed, and the key-category analysis says which categories carry
the level and which carry the variance---so that a reader knows where the evidence-graded
product is strong and where a proxy is doing the work.
