PakEmis 1.0.0rc68 reconstructs supported non-LULUCF greenhouse gases and air pollutants in Pakistan over ten fiscal years, July 2015–June 2025. This maintained technical report supports the national data-description article. Native gases, separate GWP aggregates, seven core air species, explicit source coverage and four dung/nitrogen configurations share reconciled activity and material flows. Conditional parameter uncertainty and paired trends remain separate from structural cases, missing sources and allocation uncertainty. The FY2025–26 source extension is provisional. Data, code, metadata, figures and reproduction records are prepared locally for review; external repository deposit and a persistent identifier remain pending.
