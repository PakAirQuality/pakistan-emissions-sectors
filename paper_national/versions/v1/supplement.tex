\section{Air source and process boundaries}\label{sec:airbeta}
The canonical air records distinguish source, process, category/module, species/mass convention, activity, units, dates/provenance, factor/technology applicability, control evidence, configuration, maturity, coverage, allocation support and uncertainty. The seven core species are PM$_{2.5}$, BC, NO$_x$, SO$_2$, CO, NMVOC and NH$_3$. The wider species vocabulary does not imply complete source coverage for every species. BC/OC and particulate strata are nested and cannot be summed; NO$_x$ is NO$_2$-equivalent and NH$_3$ differs from intermediate nitrogen mass.

\subsection{Public plant design evidence}
Port Qasim's generation application, PDF page 69, specifies limestone--gypsum wet FGD at no less than 92\%, bulk ESP removal of 99.7\%, low-NOx/staged-air design and proposed continuous monitoring. CIHC/China Power Hub's application, PDF page 204, specifies seawater FGD at no less than 93\%. Both original public documents are retained with retrieval hashes. These are design/proposal records, not demonstrated annual removal or measured emissions. Bulk fly-ash efficiency is not transferred to PM$_{2.5}$.

Selected-plant SO$_2$ cases use actual station consumption and credit the stated design efficiency once. Imported-coal assays remain absent; the retained fuel-quality range is explicitly a proxy. Actual bypass, availability and removal performance are unresolved. The uncontrolled and design-operation cases are neither mathematical extrema nor confidence limits and are excluded from central admission.

\subsection{Transport and industrial sensitivities}
Road factors are transferred fleet/technology averages with explicit CNG-to-LPG and class proxies. Published endpoint sensitivities retain the same class/fuel activity and do not constitute a measured technology distribution. Sulfur policy dates establish specifications rather than universal compliance. Industrial equipment and kiln-type shares remain partitions, not independently sampled multipliers. Three kiln sulfur specimens are not a nationally representative sampling distribution; fuel and raw-material sulfur, retention and removal are distinct.

\subsection{Household and nitrogen boundaries}
Reference S receives survey-supported dung. L assigns all diverted material to household combustion consistently across GHG and air products. Dung dry solids including ash and volatile solids are distinct. The gross appliance boundary lacks an outdoor escape fraction and is not ambient-ready.

The native nitrogen replay samples common livestock, crop, nutrient and applicable fate parameters, bounded where physically required. Surviving pools drive management/grazing/soil NH$_3$, NO$_x$ and N$_2$O. Product NH$_3$ factors feed the same volatilised-N pathway as indirect N$_2$O. Crop-residue threshold/intercept, management shares and excretion remain conditional structural assumptions. The complete IPCC alternative is regenerated, not mixed probabilistically with TAN.

\section{Uncertainty records}\label{sup:keycat}
Annual level and paired-change products identify configuration, native species/metric, dependence case, draw count/seed, percentile meaning and limitations. Persistent and annual-activity-error cases expose temporal dependence without subtracting unrelated annual percentiles. Waste and fugitive native reruns preserve source distributions and material constraints. Driver correlations are sensitivities, not additive variance shares.

Dated kiln, household fate, sulfur, road-factor, regional urea and plant-control cases have no assigned probabilities. Missing categories and spatial/temporal allocation uncertainty remain separate. A conditional interval cannot imply complete national source coverage.
